Rationale

Why DigiApp?

Most Grade 6 learners are focused on games and entertainment. Even skilled smartphone users may not know how to operate educational apps or basic computer tools.

DigiApp bridges the gap — turning entertainment-first habits into learning-first ones through hands-on exploration.

Learning Competency

What learners will master

Develops digital literacy through the effective, responsible, and safe use of technology — preparing learners to communicate, collaborate, and learn with digital tools.

⚠ The Problem

Grade 6 learners are skilled at entertainment apps but uncertain about educational tools and basic computer skills.

✓ The Solution

DigiApp makes learning natural — learners explore real apps the same way they use entertainment apps.

Curriculum Alignment

Supports DepEd standards — prepares learners to use technology confidently for learning and everyday tasks.

Skills Developed

  • 🌐 Online communication
  • 🤝 Digital collaboration
  • 🛡️ Digital citizenship
  • 📚 Educational app proficiency

How to use

Step 1 — Tap a badge

Orange numbered circles mark each feature. Tap one to see its explanation below the mock screen.

Step 2 — Read the explanation

The teal panel slides up with the feature name and a Grade-6 friendly description.

Step 3 — Use the arrows

← → arrows let you move through all 9 features in order. After the last one, the Task appears.
